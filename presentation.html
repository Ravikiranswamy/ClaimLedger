Problem Analysis
Executive Context

The Enterprise Brand Drift Challenge

Why generic LLMs and manual legal reviews fail modern enterprise scale.

THE VELOCITY TRAP

Explosion of Assets

Modern enterprises produce thousands of social ads, pitch decks, partner proposals, and banners weekly across dozens of decentralized marketing and sales teams.

THE LIABILITY RISK

Outdated & Conflicting Claims

Sales quotes superseded pricing (e.g., ₹799 vs official ₹999), marketing publishes unapproved performance claims, and unapproved logos or off-brand colors leak to public channels.

THE LLM FAILURE

Why AI Wrappers Fail

Pure LLMs hallucinate, produce non-deterministic verdicts across runs, and have zero understanding of institutional authority tiers (Tier 1 Master vs Tier 2 Exception).

Core Innovation
Architectural Breakthrough

Deterministic Authority Resolution

Decoupling language understanding from compliance truth.

// Institutional 6-Step Rule Execution (Pure Java) Step 1: Status Filter ➔ Discard non-approved or draft documents. Step 2: Temporal Check ➔ Check effective & expiry dates (detect outdated). Step 3: Scope Match ➔ Match product line, department & region. Step 4: Tier Hierarchy ➔ Tier 1 (Company Master) > Tier 2 (Department) > Tier 3. Step 5: Numeric Match ➔ Precise arithmetic comparator (₹799 ≠ ₹999). Step 6: Escapement ➔ Flag discrepancies to Reviewer with exact proof.

*We never ask an LLM "Is this claim compliant?". Compiled Java code deterministically computes compliance based on institutional authority hierarchy.

Dual-Stream Processing Architecture

  • Text & In-Image Extraction: Google Gemini 3.8 Flash isolates discrete factual assertions and character offsets.
  • Vector Retrieval (Neon pgvector): Sub-5ms cosine similarity lookup against 768-dim embeddings in Singapore.
  • Deterministic Rule Engine: Pure Java execution ensures 100% repeatability with zero hallucinations.
  • Append-Only Audit Ledger: Every claim, extraction, revision, and reviewer sign-off is logged chronologically.
